What these rankings do and don't mean

  • Direct plans, as you asked — and the window fits: Direct plans were created on 1 January 2013, so 2013 is the first year they can be measured at all. The 2013 column runs from each fund's Direct-plan inception (on or before 31 Jan 2013) to 31 Dec 2013.
  • Today's category labels, applied backwards. SEBI's scheme categorisation only arrived in 2017–18, and Flexi Cap was created in November 2020. For the early years a fund is sorted into the box it occupies now, which is not necessarily the mandate it ran then.
  • Median, not average. Each category-year is ranked on the median fund so one freak performer can't hand a category the crown. A category needs at least funds that year to be ranked.
  • Survivorship bias runs one way. Only funds still reporting NAV today are visible. Schemes merged or wound up along the way are missing, which flatters every historical column — most of all the early ones.
  • 2026 is incomplete — year-to-date through , not a full year.
  • Nifty 50 is a price index; fund NAVs are total-return. That hands funds a roughly 1–1.5%/yr advantage in every "beat the Nifty" count below.

So what do you actually do with this?

The grid above is history. This is the part that decides where the money goes. I tested every possible fixed blend of three to five categories for a 10,000-a-month SIP — 1,832,616 of them, weights in 1,000 steps — over the same fourteen years, and asked whether any of them can match the top three of each year.

What a fixed blend delivers

Method. The AMFI-derived mfapi.in scheme list (37,743 schemes) was filtered to Direct-plan Growth-option schemes, excluding IDCW/dividend/bonus variants — 5,028 schemes, of which fall in equity, hybrid, solution-oriented or index categories and carry usable NAV history. For each fund and each year, CY return = NAV(last day ≤ 31-Dec-Y) / NAV(last day ≤ 31-Dec-Y−1) − 1. A year is dropped for a fund if either endpoint sits more than 10 days from the year boundary, so a hole in a NAV feed can never be booked as a return. Categories are ranked within each year on median fund return, minimum funds. Nifty 50 calendar-year price returns from Yahoo Finance (^NSEI), as of .

Blend backtest. 841 of those funds carry usable month-end NAV history; a category's monthly index is the median month-on-month NAV move of its funds. Blends were built from the 19 categories with complete 2013–2026 coverage. A blend's yearly return is the contribution-weighted average of its sleeves; its corpus is the exact sum of independent monthly SIPs, since a split that never changes leaves each sleeve independent — no simulation is involved. All 1,832,616 blends of three to five categories in 1,000 steps were enumerated over 164 monthly contributions, January 2013 to August 2026.

Educational analysis of public NAV data — not investment advice. A category's past calendar year says nothing reliable about its next one, which is precisely what the grid above shows.
