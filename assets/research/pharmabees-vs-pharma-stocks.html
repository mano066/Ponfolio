Persistence — can you pick?

Still mostly no. With one exception that has now held for six periods running.

Rank correlation between consecutive periods averages +0.14. The leaderboard reshuffles: Sun Pharma led 2021 and 2022, Aurobindo took 2023 with +150%, Glenmark 2024 with +89%, Laurus 2025 and 2026. Chasing last period's winner would have been a treadmill.

Torrent Pharma beat the fund in all six periods — 6/6, 28.3% a year, and its worst period was only −4%. That is the single genuine case for owning a stock instead. The obstacle is price, not evidence: it trades at a P/E of 78.9 and price-to-book of 19.9.

Warnings

One caveat on the window, one on the entry.

Five years is a short record, and a kind one

This window contains one bad year and four good ones. It excludes 2016–2019, when pharma fell four years running. Judge the fund on this evidence by all means — but do not read 13.3% a year as a durable expectation from a sector that has had much worse stretches.

You would be buying at the top of the range

PHARMABEES closed at 100% of its 52-week range, 14.0% above its 200-day average, with the pharma-to-Nifty ratio at the 100th percentile of five years. A ten-month SIP is the right mitigation — but do not front-load it.

Contraindications

Both sides, on this window's evidence only.

Reasons to own the ETF

  1. Beat the median constituent — 13.3% against 12.8%.
  2. Worst drawdown −21.1% against the average stock's −40.4%.
  3. Only 7 of 16 stocks beat it; in 2022 only 5 did.
  4. Lowest volatility of any pharma line at 15.1%; 6th of 18 on Sharpe.
  5. Defensive sector with low correlation to a broad, cyclical-heavy equity portfolio, so a small allocation can trim overall volatility.

Reasons not to

  1. Torrent beat it in all six periods, at 28.3% a year.
  2. 54.6% of the fund sits in ten names — concentrated for an index.
  3. 21.3% is Sun Pharma, so this is substantially one bet already.
  4. Five years is a short and flattering sample.
  5. Buying at 100% of the 52-week range and a five-year relative high.

In plain English: measured only against what it has actually done, the fund earns its place — mid-pack return for materially less damage, and better than the market. The case for a stock rests on Torrent alone, and Torrent's price already reflects it. Take the ETF, stage it in, and revisit Torrent if it de-rates.
