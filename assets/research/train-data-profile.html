The return distribution is sharply peaked with fat tails and a slightly negative median (−0.09%): most stock-days in a 2,000-name universe drift down while a thin tail carries the gains. The ±20% end bars are India's circuit limit doing its job. The factor chart's tall cluster at 100.3 is the 2:1 split — the most common Indian corporate action.

Universe breadth over time

In panel Eligible to trade Eligibility needs 200 sessions of history, so it begins 2010-10

The panel grows steadily from 1,320 to ~1,600 names. The tradeable subset is far smaller and far more volatile — 196 names in 2013-08 against 691 in 2018-01, a 3.5× swing driven by turnover, not by listings. Any strategy taking a fixed top-5 per day is selecting from a pool whose size moves by that much.

Median price falls from ₹64 (2010) to ₹37 (2013) then triples to ₹104 by 2018 — the smallcap cycle. Daily return volatility runs 2.9–4.1%, peaking in 2016. A model trained across this window sees several genuinely different regimes, which is the argument for walk-forward validation rather than a random split.

Masking flags

what is excluded, and what is deliberately kept

0.28% of returns are NaN (9,869 rows) — the sum of the masking flags. big_move is the deliberate exception: 1,873 unexplained large steps stay in as real returns, because masking them would delete YESBANK's 2020 collapse and ADANIENT's 2023 drop and flatter every strategy measured here.
