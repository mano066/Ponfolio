Promotion criteria — all must hold

  • ✓Beats v4 on live-forward paper data after costs — not just in backtest.
  • ✓Holds lower or comparable max drawdown through the validation window.
  • ✓Edge survives the survivorship-free re-run (bias removed).
  • ✓Sufficient independent trades; no dependence on a few outliers.
  • ✓v4 remains an instant fallback the moment v5 misbehaves.

Why staged, not a straight swap: v5's edge is risk management (volatility-target sizing + regime gating), not better stock-picking — the robust kind of edge, but still unvalidated live. All metrics are measured on a survivorship-biased daily universe, so absolute returns are overstated; only the relative v4→v5 improvement (drawdown, Calmar) is trustworthy until the rebuild. v5 is bull-gated — proven to stand aside in bad regimes, not proven to trade them. Research-only; no production code, paper history, or git state changes until the gate is cleared.
