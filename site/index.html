About

Institutional-grade analysis, on your own desktop.

Ponfolio was built to give individual investors the kind of decision support usually locked inside trading desks — without the noise. Every signal shows its reasoning: why it's valid, where to buy, where the stop sits, and when to step aside.

  • Evidence over hypeEvery setup is backed by RSI, MACD, ADX, Bollinger, volume and support/resistance — not vague tips.
  • Local-first & privateYour data and API sessions stay on your machine. Nothing is sold or shared.
  • One place for your wealthStocks, mutual funds, SIPs and everyday expenses — tracked together.

Features

Everything you need to trade and track wealth.

A single terminal that replaces a dozen scattered tools and spreadsheets.

AI Swing Scanner

Scans the Nifty Total Market for the best setups and shows exact buy zones, stops, targets, risk:reward and a plain-English reason for each.

Portfolio Analytics

Live holdings, P&L, and allocation with Zerodha Kite sync — see exactly where you stand at a glance.

Mutual Funds & SIP

Track your MF portfolio and model SIPs with a built-in simulator to project long-term outcomes.

Wealth & Expenses

Multi-currency cashflow tracking — income, expenses, savings rate and closing balances, all reconciled.

Market Signals

Deep technical signals across trend, momentum, volume and volatility — with a transparent 0–100 strength score.

Private by Design

A local desktop app — your broker session and data never leave your computer. No cloud lock-in.
